## Welcome to Health-RI Data Stewardship Training Hub

This platform hosts training materials and eLearning resources developed by Health-RI in collaboration with training coordinators at the network nodes.

### Available Training

**[Data Stewards Onboarding in the Health-RI Network](onboarding-overview)**

A comprehensive programme covering roles, responsibilities, networks, and professional development for data stewards.

### About This Platform

Our training materials are:
- Developed collaboratively across the Health-RI Network
- Focused on practical skills and knowledge
- Open and accessible to everyone, but targeted for Health RI network members
- Continuously updated based on community feedback

### Getting Started

Explore the available training modules using the sidebar navigation, or jump directly to the [Data Stewards Onboarding Overview](onboarding-overview).
